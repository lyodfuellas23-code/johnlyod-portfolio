About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate with a background that has been connected to computers long before I started learning how to code. Growing up around our family computer shop, I spent much of my childhood playing computer games and eventually started helping run the shop from Grade 8 through college. Despite being familiar with computers, I originally wanted to pursue Psychology and even tried to avoid taking a computer-related course. However, after not being accepted into my preferred school, I eventually chose Computer Science and discovered programming during my first year of college.

While programming was not something I initially planned to pursue, I found myself enjoying the process of building and figuring things out. I learn best through hands-on experience and practical applications, where I can take what I learn and use it to create something useful rather than focusing solely on theory. I'm naturally curious and enjoy exploring different tools and technologies, and I'm currently developing my skills in front-end web development with the long-term goal of becoming a full-stack web developer.
